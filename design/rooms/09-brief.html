The handoff

Six blocks. It opens and closes in the author's voice; everything between is operational. The ring never fills — ILC doesn't cross into execution with you.

the idea execution — yours
01 · the bet

You are betting that a bookkeeper will route client paper through a stranger's address before she'll hire another pair of hands — the friction is real, the trust is unproven, and everything below exists to test that one sentence.

02 · prove next
the evidence to get

One bookkeeping firm forwarding a live client inbox for a full monthly close — not a demo, not a pilot deck.

the bar

She does it a second month without being asked. Repeat, unprompted, is the whole bar.

03 · the order · each step gated by the one before it
01
Stand up the mail spine — receive, parse, return filed.
produces

A working address that answers with a coded CSV.

signal to continue

Your own test inbox survives 100 mixed receipts.

02
Put it in one bookkeeper's hands — free, for one close.
produces

The first live hand-off; the trust question answered in practice.

signal to continue

She forwards month two unprompted — the prove-next bar.

03
Price the second firm before polishing anything.
produces

A number a stranger agreed to — demand's missing rung.

signal to continue

Paid, not promised. Then the loop re-reads.

04 · what won't count
  • Sign-ups, stars, or upvotes — attention is not intake.
  • Founder friends testing it — their trust isn't the trust in question.
  • Parsing accuracy wins — the evidence already says the build isn't the bet.
05 · the gates
revise if

The firm forwards, but only after you chase — the friction was yours, not hers. Return the target clause to Evolve.

stop if

Three firms decline for the same named reason. That reason becomes evidence — the loop keeps it either way.

06 · the handoff

Over to you — it's yours now. The read holds until the world moves; the watch is set, and the loop stays open behind you.
