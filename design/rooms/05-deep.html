Ⅰ · the read

The friction is real and the build is cheap — which is exactly why the bet lands on distribution, where the evidence is thinnest.

Email-native intake removes the adoption step every retrieved competitor stumbles on, and the parsing itself is commodity. What the evidence cannot yet show is a repeatable path to the bookkeeper's desk: no named channel, no priced precedent for intake alone, and a do-nothing baseline that costs nothing. What stands is the wedge; what has to be true is that someone will pay for it before trust exists.
