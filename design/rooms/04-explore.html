Ⅰ · our read

You're describing a hand-off problem, not a scanning problem — the pain lives at the moment a shoebox of paper becomes someone else's job. That someone is more findable than the crowd.

what's already clear
  • The friction is documented where bookkeepers talk — r/bookkeeping threads name it monthly.
  • OCR itself is commodity — the build is not the bet.
still open
  • Who pays — the bookkeeper who suffers, or the client who causes it?
  • Whether trust in handing over an inbox can be earned by software at all.
branchability

Rich — the seed splits cleanly along positioning, mechanism, and target. Three strands hold under the evidence.
