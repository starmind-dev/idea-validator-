Cold Light

One field. One fiber of light. Two apertures. Nine rooms. ILC is a serious workspace — white, cool, exact. One persistent idea holds many states of thought without losing its identity or its history; the interface shows one state at a time, and the light marks where thinking is happening now.

04 · the main shape

One idea, many states — shown one at a time

The idea is a fiber of light. Diffuse at rough, gathering at explore, focused to a core at deep, interfering at compare, ringed at re-evaluate, sealed at brief. The full spectrum appears together only where it IS the content — here, on the landing, and in lineage. Inside the workspace, the state lens shows one lit state.

07 · the grammar

Motion is orientation, never judgment

One motion per navigation, direction invariant — widening opens, pressure settles, the thread slides, compare splits, lineage zooms. Identical for every idea, whatever its numbers. Click to replay.

the pressure room

Does this hold?

Same sheet, same field — only the motion of arrival differs. 160–240ms, ease-out, then stillness. Reduced motion collapses every entrance to a crossfade.

Rules: 160–240ms, one motion per navigation, no choreography chains. Something always survives the cut — the state lens inside an idea, the rail between global rooms, the idea's title everywhere. Zero-context hard cuts are forbidden.
