Ink & Field

One field. One thread. Two apertures. Nine rooms. The UI's subject is not the answer — it is the motion of thought around an idea. Paper and ink, because ILC is an analyst's dossier, not a machine dashboard. The page is written, not generated.

01 · the mark

A line that loops once, and continues

The thread enters, loops, and leaves the frame uncut. Never a closed circle — the loop never closes. This is the only place besides the spine and the thread bar where the red thread exists.

03 · the language

Three voices

Who is speaking is always legible from the type alone. The analyst writes in serif. The product speaks in sans. The machine reports in mono. No voice borrows another's register.

Author
Literata · 400–600
Verdict prose, the bet, the handoff, editorial nudges. The analyst speaks.
The evidence doesn't yet support strong demand — and that is a statement about evidence you can change, not a verdict on reality.
Interface
IBM Plex Sans · 400–600
Chrome, body copy, controls. The product speaks.
Explore widens a rough idea into angles — no score, no rank, no verdict. Deep pressures one version against real market, monetization, and originality evidence. They're different jobs, not a short version and a long one.

04 · the main shape

The thread, at its three scales

One line of thought runs through the whole product. It appears at exactly three scales — the spine, the thread bar, the mark — and nowhere else. Restraint is the style.

scale 1 · the thread bar

An idea's own loop, on one line

Lives at the top of every room while inside an idea. Every filled stop is real and clickable; dashed segments are the idea's open future. Moving between rooms is moving along this line — the line never breaks.

05 · the apertures

One field, two registers

Explore and Deep are not two brands — they are the same attention at two apertures. The ground barely shifts temperature; what changes is density: type weight, leading, whitespace. Widening breathes. Pressure condenses.

open air · explore

What could this become?

Cool ink. Lighter weight. Wide leading, wide margins — the page opens its aperture. Nothing here is ranked; the fan is drawn in thin, even strands.

neutral · desk, shelves

Where was I?

Plain paper. The resting register — calm, even, unhurried. Memory rooms don't lean toward either aperture.

pressure · deep

Does this hold?

Warm graphite. Heavier ink, tighter leading, denser columns — the page condenses around one version of the idea. Same paper, aperture closed.

07 · the grammar

Motion is orientation, never judgment

One motion per navigation. Direction is invariant — widening always opens, pressure always settles, the thread always slides horizontally, lineage always zooms. Identical for every idea, whatever its numbers. Click to replay.

the pressure room

Does this hold?

Same sheet, same field — only the motion of arrival differs. 160–240ms, ease-out, then stillness. Reduced motion collapses every entrance to a crossfade.

Rules: 160–240ms, one motion per navigation, no choreography chains. Something always survives the cut — the thread bar inside an idea, the rail between global rooms, the idea's title everywhere. Zero-context hard cuts are forbidden.

What this system will not be
